Engineering note

Legal-grade markers are only added when a real source states a grade value. A marker may be placed at the documented site/address when the source does not provide the exact City Map point coordinate; those markers are explicitly identified as approximate display locations. Datum is never assumed. Verify controlling legal grades against the current City Map / Borough Topographical records before design or filing.
